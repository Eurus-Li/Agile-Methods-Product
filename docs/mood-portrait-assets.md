# Hand-painted Mood Portraits

Five expression portraits in a matching style were generated with the built-in image_gen, using `web/assets/images/rongrong-cutout.png` as the character reference. The original assets are kept; at runtime the new images are loaded into Quick Moods on Home / Reply. The circular display size stays at 44px; the images are decorative only, and the button labels still carry the mood semantics. After the templates are regenerated, app.js still applies this replacement.

## calm

Asset: `web/assets/images/mood-calm-painted.png`

```text
Use case: identity-preserve. Edit/reference image: Rongrong cutout puppy. Create one square head-and-shoulders mood avatar of this SAME fluffy cream-white puppy, floppy ears, chocolate button eyes and nose. Match the soft handmade gouache/watercolor brushwork and paper grain of the reference, clearly hand-painted, not photographic or 3D. Face centered, large and readable at 44px, full ears inside frame with 10% margin, consistent straight-on framing. Warm pale cream #FBF3EC paper background. No text, symbols, props, accessories, borders or watermarks. Expression: peaceful relaxed eyes gently closed, tiny content closed-mouth smile.
```
